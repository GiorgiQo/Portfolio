WHAT I CAN HELP WITH

Editing, sound, and the finishing touches.

I work in DaVinci Resolve, from the first cut to the final export. Here’s what I can bring to your project.

Video editing
Selecting the right shots, building the story, and setting a pace that keeps viewers interested.
Color correction & grading
Balancing exposure and color, matching shots, and creating a consistent look that fits the mood.
Audio editing & mixing
Reducing background noise, improving dialogue, and balancing voices, music, and sound effects.
Subtitles & captions
Adding clear, well-timed text so your video is easy to follow, even with the sound off.
Transitions & visual effects
Connecting scenes and adding effects where they help the video, without distracting from the story.
Fusion & motion graphics
Creating animated titles, graphic elements, and visual effects that suit your video’s style.
Reels & Shorts
Editing vertical videos with a strong opening, clear captions, and pacing suited to social media.
Multicam editing
Syncing footage from several cameras and choosing the best angle as the action unfolds.
